# 1920 workflow retirement

The temporary direct-high-resolution profiles and derived geometry were removed after live profiling showed the full-resolution detector could not maintain the required freshness on this machine. The application now captures the game display and normalizes it into the supported 720×404 analysis geometry.

The historical `assist_1920` and `live_v6_1920` recordings were moved under `output/archive/retired_1920/` for traceability. They are not runnable profiles or current evidence of the supported 720 workflow.
